title: Blood pressure and cholesterol in diabetes

High blood pressure and high LDL cholesterol increase the risk of heart attack, stroke, and kidney disease in people with diabetes.
A common blood pressure target is below 130/80 mmHg if it can be reached safely. Home readings should be taken seated after 5 minutes of rest, with the arm supported at heart level.
Many adults with diabetes aged 40-75 are prescribed a statin. LDL targets are individualized by the clinician according to overall cardiovascular risk.
ACE inhibitors and ARBs protect the kidneys, and potassium and kidney function are checked after starting them. Combining them with spironolactone or NSAIDs increases the risk of high potassium and kidney problems.
