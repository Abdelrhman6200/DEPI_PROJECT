---
title: Eye and kidney screening in diabetes
source: Prototype KB - summary of common screening schedules

Diabetic eye disease often has no symptoms early. A dilated eye examination or retinal photography is recommended at diagnosis of type 2 diabetes and then every 1-2 years if normal, or more often if retinopathy is present.
Kidney screening includes a urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) and a blood test for eGFR at least once a year. A UACR of 30 mg/g or higher on repeated tests indicates kidney damage.
Blood pressure and cholesterol checks are part of routine diabetes care, and a dental check-up at least yearly is advised.
