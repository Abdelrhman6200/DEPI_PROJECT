---
title: Physical activity and diabetes
source: Prototype KB - summary of common activity guidance
version: 2026.1
language: en
audience: patient
---
Most adults with type 2 diabetes benefit from at least 150 minutes per week of moderate activity, such as brisk walking, spread over at least 3 days, plus muscle-strengthening activity 2-3 times a week. Breaking up long periods of sitting every 30 minutes also helps.
A short walk after meals lowers the rise in glucose after eating.
People taking insulin or sulfonylureas should check glucose before and after longer activity and carry fast sugar, because activity can cause lows, sometimes hours later. People with foot problems, retinopathy, or heart disease should ask their care team which activities are safe.
