HbA1c reflects average blood glucose over roughly the previous 2-3 months. It is usually checked every 3 months when treatment is changing or targets are not met, and about every 6 months when stable.
A common target for many non-pregnant adults is below 7 percent, but the target is individualized. Older adults, people with frequent hypoglycemia, or people with other serious conditions often have a less strict target such as below 8 percent.
HbA1c can be misleading in anemia, recent blood loss or transfusion, pregnancy, and advanced kidney disease. CGM metrics can complement HbA1c.
